      Calendar

      Off unless you give it a calendar. With one, each event of the coming week stands on the route at its minute as a reporting point, named with five letters from its title.

      Google Calendar: Settings, your calendar, “Secret address in iCal format”. iCloud: share the calendar as a public calendar and copy its link. Outlook: Settings, Shared calendars, Publish, the ICS link.

      A watch face cannot read the phone's calendar itself, so the phone fetches this link every three hours while the face is running. The link stays on this phone; only each event's time and five-letter name go to the watch. All-day events are left out.
