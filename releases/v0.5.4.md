# Groundtrack 0.5.4

- **The face opens again quickly.** Leaving the watch face for the timeline or the launcher used to mean the whole map was drawn again from the start on return. The watch now keeps the map's ground for the hour's chart in its storage and takes it back: in the emulator, Groundtrack's chart is ready about four times sooner, and Fuller's in about half the time. The first opening of each hour still makes the chart (and keeps it, a little longer than before); the ones after it do not.
- Nothing else changes on screen. Both watch faces are built smaller, which makes room for it.
