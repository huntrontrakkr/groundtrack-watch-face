# Groundtrack 0.8.0

- **A flick moves to the next satellite.** Following more than one satellite, flick your wrist (or flick twice, a setting) and a track list comes up over the chart: each satellite with its symbol and its view, the highlight dropping to the next while the old chart breaks up behind it; then the new chart comes in under a reacquiring sweep, SIGNAL ACQUIRED. Following one, a flick runs a scan as before. The phone follows the change.
- **The phone's antenna** beside the battery: lit while the phone is in reach, dimmed when it is not. A double pulse when the phone disconnects and, new, when a satellite rises over home's horizon, both off in Quiet Time; the animations can hold in Quiet Time too.
- **The settings page, rebuilt**: a chart room's dark panels, the preview held at the top with a slider through the hour and the Time 2 or the Round 2 at a touch, galleries of every plate, rim style and figure set drawn by the watch's own code, each choice a segmented control or a switch, and Defaults beside Save.
- The hour's scan runs once an hour, not again when the chart is redrawn; new data waits for an animation to finish.

Includes both Groundtrack and Groundtrack Fuller for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.
