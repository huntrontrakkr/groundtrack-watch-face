# Groundtrack 0.4.3

- **An hour near a pole is no longer smeared.** On the hour chart, a slow satellite passing close to the North or South Pole had the land drawn out into stripes, because the flat chart's meridians close up there. Such an hour is now drawn on the world band, as an hour too long for the chart already is. The band stops at 84°, so right over the pole the satellite sits at its edge. Groundtrack Fuller is unchanged: its faces have no pole.
