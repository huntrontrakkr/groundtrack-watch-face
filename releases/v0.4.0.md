# Groundtrack 0.4.0

The watch's own state, on every plate.

- **The fuel line.** Along the top edge, a gauge of the battery: filled to its charge in the plate's route ink, ticked at the quarters like a fuel gauge, in the plate's warning ink at 20% and under, in its lettering ink while charging. It is drawn in each plate's own inks, so it belongs to all thirty-two.
- **The link.** When the phone goes out of reach the fuel line breaks into dashes, as a navigator draws a position without a fix, and the corner says NO LINK.
- **Vibrate when the phone is out of reach.** A new setting, off unless you turn it on: a double pulse when the link drops, never in quiet time.
- The corner no longer says BAT when the battery is low: the fuel line says it.
