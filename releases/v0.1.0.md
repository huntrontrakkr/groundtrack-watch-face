# Groundtrack 0.1.0

The first build for a real watch (Pebble Time 2, emery). Until now both faces have run only in the emulator.

Two watch faces:

- **Groundtrack** (`groundtrack-enroute.pbw`): the Sun, the Moon and GPS on the hour chart, QZSS on its whole day, and the ISS, Tiangong, Hubble, Landsat 9 and NOAA-20 on the world band.
- **Groundtrack Fuller** (`groundtrack-fuller.pbw`): the same bodies on a rolling Fuller sheet.

Each draws its hour's chart on the watch from data the phone sends days ahead, and needs the phone only to refill it. Settings (body, plate, figures, readout, home, events) are on the face's settings page, with a preview drawn by the watch's own code.

What to watch for on hardware, none of which the emulator can show:

- The app's stack is 2 KB and the emulator does not enforce its guard. The log line "stack N of 2016" (with each chart built) says how deep it has been; in the emulator it reads 1,356 to 1,364.
- How long a chart takes to build on the watch's own processor (about a second of work in the emulator).
- The settings page, about 210 to 230 KB as a data URL, in the phone app's web view.
- Battery over a day.
