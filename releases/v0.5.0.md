# Groundtrack 0.5.0

- **Polar charts.** An hour chart that would reach within ten degrees of a pole is now drawn on a polar projection, turned so north is up at its middle, instead of a flat chart that drew the land out in stripes there. The hours over a pole stay on the chart rather than going to the world band: GLONASS's hours over the Arctic now show Alaska and the Arctic coast, and a satellite found at CelesTrak that crosses either pole is charted across it. The compass rose turns to true north.
- **The map reaches both poles.** It now covers the whole Earth, Antarctica included, with a map of each polar region for the polar charts.
- Groundtrack Fuller is unchanged on screen, and a few hundred bytes smaller.
