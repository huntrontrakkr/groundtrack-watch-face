# Groundtrack 0.3.1

The minutes, where the chart already says the hour.

- **Minutes with the hour figure.** A new readout for the hour chart and the Fuller sheet: this hour's large figure becomes the time, its minutes beside it in whichever figures you have chosen (smaller, outlined, in the route's ink, Departure Mono, or with a colon), at 40 px. The next hour's outlined figure keeps its place, or steps to the other side of the route where the time would reach it. It reads like the counters above a control room's board, in the chart's own type.
- **The minute ticker of 0.3.0 is withdrawn.** A fixed panel pasted onto the map, in scaled-up lettering, with a meter the route already gives. A watch set to it comes up with the minute flag.
- The world band and the whole-day charts keep their own time; a bare hour chart its time in full beside the body.
