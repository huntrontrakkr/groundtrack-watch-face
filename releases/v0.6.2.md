# Groundtrack v0.6.2

One satellite picker for every role. Check satellites in the shared catalog or CelesTrak search to add companions; the selected cards clearly distinguish the primary route from companion markers. Make primary swaps roles without losing a selected satellite. Replace uses the same catalog and custom lookup for all three positions, with an explicit target and Cancel.

Custom satellites remain available under Your satellites. Existing selections carry over, and the live preview shows changes before Save. The limit remains one primary and two companions.
