# Groundtrack 0.3.2

Eight more plates, and the plates in an order that makes sense.

- **Night Side.** The Earth from orbit at night: by day a dim blue sea and teal land, at night both black with the coastlines lit gold.
- **Infrared.** The colour tables forecasters lay over weather-satellite infrared: violet lowland through orange to pale-yellow peaks, on a black sea.
- **Planetary.** The airbrushed Mars and Moon charts of the Apollo and Viking years, drawn over the Earth: ochre and salmon relief, a blue route.
- **Moonlit.** Airbrushed relief by moonlight: silver and slate on black. The dark companion to Airbrush and Planetary.
- **Vector.** No fills at all: the world in line work, one pale beam on black.
- **Graphite.** Spare grey instruments, the route the one white line.
- **Star Atlas.** Navy paper, cream coastlines, a pale gold route, after the engraved star atlases navigators used.
- **Rodger Young.** A fleet's tactical chart: khaki ground, a gunmetal sea, the line of advance in red.
- **The plates are listed like with like:** paper charts, shaded relief, blue line drawings, tracking boards, mission control's glass, single-ink displays, the Earth from space. A plate you had chosen stays chosen.
