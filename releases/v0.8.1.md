# Groundtrack 0.8.1

- **One switch for all the animations**, at the top of the Animations settings. Off, nothing moves but the minute (a flick still moves to the next satellite, plainly).
- **A flick with one satellite** no longer brings up the reacquiring's LOSS OF SIGNAL box over the time just as the light comes on: it runs one of the other scans.
- **Switching satellites is smoother**: the track list drops and scrolls with the watch to itself, and the new chart is built while it says ACQUIRING.
- **Companions' dotted tracks** are drawn in the marks' ink, as the companion's own mark is, instead of the grid's, where they were lost in the map.

Includes both Groundtrack and Groundtrack Fuller for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.
