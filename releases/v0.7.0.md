# Groundtrack 0.7.0

- **Pebble Round 2.** Groundtrack (Enroute and the Plotboard) and Groundtrack Fuller now run on the Round 2, laid out for the round screen. Choose how the edge is lettered: **Bezel** (the margins round an octagon, the diagonals in a turned version of the face's own lettering), **Chords** (no bezel: the map runs to the edge, the lettering in straight lines over it) or **Instrument** (the bezel with the minutes ticked down its sides). The Plotboard's minutes run round an arc, each hour's figure at its own place on the scale; the battery is a bar round the top.
- **Animations**, each with its own switch in the new Animations section of the settings, where ▶ plays any of them on the preview:
  - the body blinks as it steps each minute;
  - home pulses at the minute a satellite rises into view;
  - scans: a **refresh** line with a phosphor glow, a **pixel sort** sweeping the screen, **reacquire** (the picture breaks into sorted noise and resolves again under a blinking LOSS OF SIGNAL box) and a **tear**;
  - the scans run as each hour begins, at random minutes once to four times an hour, and at a flick of the wrist; on the screen-like plates, or every plate if you choose.
- The Time 2 apps carry their chart drawing as loadable code, leaving much more memory free for the charts.

Includes both Groundtrack and Groundtrack Fuller for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.
