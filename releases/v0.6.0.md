# Groundtrack 0.6.0

- **Track more than one satellite.** Both watchfaces can show two additional satellites as numbered markers, updated every minute. Choose Satellite 1 and Satellite 2 under Additional satellites in settings. The primary body still determines the map, route, clock and home pass forecast. Additional markers appear when their ground points are within the map; Plotboard offers the widest coverage. Their orbit data is cached for about a day and refreshed when the phone is available. An unavailable companion never stops the primary clock.
- **True north on Fuller triangles.** An optional, compact star-tipped north line follows the USGS cartographic convention for geographic true north. Each mark uses its own triangle's orientation at the centre, where that centre is visible. It uses the selected plate's ink and sits beneath the route and time markings. Enable True-north arrows in Triangle orientation; it is off by default.
- Existing preferences survive the upgrade. Satellite storage is separate for each selected object, and the settings preview uses the same map renderer as the watch.
