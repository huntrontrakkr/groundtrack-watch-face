# Groundtrack 0.6.3

- Each companion now has its own **Show dotted track** checkbox, off by default. Subdued dots show its ground track for the current hour while the primary route and clock remain prominent.
- Track choices follow satellites through role swaps and survive Save and reopen. All three positions still share the catalog and custom CelesTrak lookup.
- Fuller’s optional true-north stars are smaller and rotate with their stems, so a star point follows the local north direction on each triangle.

Includes both Groundtrack and Groundtrack Fuller for Pebble Time 2.
